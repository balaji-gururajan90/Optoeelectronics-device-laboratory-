SPUTTERONE
Enabling Process Digital Twin

A Physics-Informed Interactive Magnetron Sputtering Simulator

SputterOne is an interactive simulation platform for real-time exploration of magnetron sputtering process conditions. It integrates sputtering yield, gas-phase transport, reactive sputtering, co-sputtering, and thin-film growth mode models within a unified environment.

Developed by Dr. Balaji Gururajan at the Optoelectronics Device Laboratory, Yuan Ze University.

Users

Who May Find SputterOne Useful

Students and Learners

  • Explore the basic principles of magnetron sputtering interactively.
  • Observe how pressure influences gas-phase transport.
  • Compare approximate sputtering-yield trends for different targets.
  • Examine how material and substrate choices affect predicted growth modes.
  • Build preliminary process understanding before laboratory training.

Educators

  • Demonstrate relationships between common sputtering parameters.
  • Supplement lectures on physical vapour deposition and thin-film growth.
  • Introduce sputtering yield, transport, and reactive sputtering concepts.
  • Use simulations as discussion exercises for students.
  • Support introductory laboratory preparation and self-learning activities.

Researchers and Faculty

  • Perform preliminary exploration of selected sputtering conditions.
  • Compare qualitative trends across available materials and substrates.
  • Examine the behaviour of the integrated reduced-order models.
  • Use simulation results to support early-stage process discussions.
  • Provide feedback that can guide future model development and experimental calibration.
Usage note: SputterOne is intended as an educational and preliminary exploration tool. Simulation results should be interpreted qualitatively and validated experimentally before use in process design.
