SPUTTERONE
Enabling Process Digital Twin

A Physics-Informed Interactive Magnetron Sputtering Simulator

SputterOne is an interactive simulation platform for real-time exploration of magnetron sputtering process conditions. It integrates sputtering yield, gas-phase transport, reactive sputtering, co-sputtering, and thin-film growth mode models within a unified environment.

Developed by Dr. Balaji Gururajan at the Optoelectronics Device Laboratory, Yuan Ze University.

Who Can Use SputterOne

Researchers and Scientists

  • Explore magnetron sputtering conditions before conducting experimental runs.
  • Understand how working pressure affects gas-phase transport regimes.
  • Compare sputtering yield trends across different target materials.
  • Examine how substrate selection influences thin-film growth modes.
  • Evaluate new material and substrate combinations during initial process planning.

Educators and Students

  • Use SputterOne as a virtual laboratory for thin-film deposition courses.
  • Explore sputtering yield and pressure-dependent transport interactively.
  • Study reactive sputtering hysteresis and co-sputtering composition.
  • Complete structured tutorials and laboratory learning modules.
  • Develop physical intuition before working with sputtering equipment.

Simulation and Computational Researchers

  • Study reduced-order and physics-informed models of thin-film processes.
  • Examine the Yamamura-Bohdansky sputtering yield implementation.
  • Explore kinetic-theory transport and Berg reactive sputtering models.
  • Investigate Bauer growth-mode and multi-model integration methods.
  • Use the MIT-licensed codebase as a foundation for process digital twin development.
