SputterOne

A Physics-Informed Interactive Magnetron Sputtering Simulator

SputterOne is an interactive simulation platform for real-time exploration of magnetron sputtering process conditions. It integrates sputtering yield, gas-phase transport, reactive sputtering, co-sputtering, and thin-film growth mode models within a unified environment. Developed by Dr. Balaji Gururajan at the Optoelectronics Device Laboratory, Yuan Ze University.

Key Features

Physics-Guided Process Exploration

9

9 Target Materials

Cu, Al, Ti, Zn, Sn, Ag, W, Ta, Mo with Yamamura-Bohdansky yield model.

3

3 Substrate Options

Si(100), Glass, Sapphire with Bauer free energy criterion.

Ph

Physics-Informed

Discharge model, Berg reactive sputtering, transport regime classification.

AI

Process Advisory

Integrated physics-informed parameter pre-screening before simulation.
