October 31 - November 5, 2026

Six Research Presentations at IUMRS-ICA 2026, Tainan

The Optoelectronics Device Laboratory presented one oral paper and five poster papers at IUMRS-ICA 2026, held at ICC Tainan, Taiwan.

Excellent Poster Award

Venkatesh Narasimhan received the Excellent Poster Award at IUMRS-ICA 2026 in recognition of his research presentation.

Oral Presentation (11:40-11:55)

Interface-Engineered CuAlO2/MgZnO All-Oxide Heterojunction for Visible-Blind Deep-UV Photodetection

Authors: Balaji Gururajan, Venkatesh Narasimhan, Deveshwar Sasikumar, Wei Sheng Liu
Presenter: Dr. Balaji Gururajan

Poster P-1-021

Phase Evolution from Bi2S3 to Ternary Cu3BiS3 Thin Films via RF Magnetron Sputtered Metallic Precursor Stacking and Sulfurization

Authors: Venkatesh Narasimhan, Balaji Gururajan, Wei-Sheng Liu
Presenter: Venkatesh Narasimhan

Poster P-1-024

Integrated SCAPS-1D and Machine Learning Study of Novel Absorber Materials for Terrestrial and Space Photovoltaics

Authors: Balaji Gururajan, Venkatesh Narasimhan, Rafly Arya Permana, Nguyen Dai Vi, Danielle Sarah Charles, Wei-Sheng Liu
Presenter: Venkatesh Narasimhan

Poster P-3-236

Sputtered CuGaO2 Thin Films as p-Type Transparent Conducting Oxides for Ultraviolet Optoelectronics

Authors: Vaishnavi Ekhande, Deveshwar Sasikumar, Balaji Gururajan, Wei-Sheng Liu
Presenter: Vaishnavi Ekhande

Poster P-3-237

Process Parameter Optimization of Zn2SnO4 Ternary Oxide Thin Films Deposited Using Co-Sputtering

Authors: Pratik D Ughade, Venkatesh Narasimhan, Balaji Gururajan, Wei Sheng Liu
